# ATKT (Allowed To Keep Terms) Rules (illustrative)

- A student who fails in some courses (heads) of Semesters I and II can take admission to the Second Year if the number of failed heads is within the university limit (up to 4 heads in a year).
- To take admission to the Third Year (TE), all First Year (Sem I and II) courses must be cleared.
- To take admission to the Final Year (BE), all Second Year (Sem III and IV) courses must be cleared.
- Backlog courses are cleared by appearing in the exam in the next odd/even cycle along with regular students.
- Backlog exam form fee: Rs. 450 per head (theory), Rs. 300 per practical.
- Maximum duration to complete the degree: 8 years (twice the program length).
- Grade improvement: Allowed once for up to 2 courses per semester, within 1 year of declaration of results.
- ATKT students can attend lectures of the higher class but must clear backlogs before the next progression requirement.
