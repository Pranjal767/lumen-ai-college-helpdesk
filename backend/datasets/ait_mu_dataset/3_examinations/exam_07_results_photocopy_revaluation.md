# Results and Revaluation
## Results
Results are declared by MU, normally within 30 to 45 days of the last paper. Expected for December 2026 exams: by 22 January 2027. Check on the MU results portal and AIT Student Portal.

## Photocopy and Revaluation (two-step)
1. Apply for photocopy of the answer book within 5 days of result declaration. Fee: Rs. 500 per subject (ESE theory only).
2. Photocopy is provided online in about 5 days. Review it.
3. Apply for revaluation within 5 days of getting the photocopy. Fee: Rs. 700 per subject.
4. Revised results are declared in about 30 days. Marks are changed only if the difference is 10% or more of the maximum marks (or as per MU rule).

## Verification of Marks
Only for marks totalling errors; fee Rs. 300 per subject.

## Re-assessment of Term Work
Not allowed. Contact the Head of Department for internal assessment grievances.
