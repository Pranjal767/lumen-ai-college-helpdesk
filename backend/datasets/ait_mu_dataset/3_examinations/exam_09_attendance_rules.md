# Attendance (as per University of Mumbai and AICTE)
- Minimum 75% attendance in theory and practical for each semester, counted separately for each course.
- Attendance is marked in the ERP and visible on the Student Portal; it is updated every Monday.
- 65% to 74%: eligible for condonation by Principal with valid reasons (medical certificate, MU/State level participation). Condonation fee: Rs. 500 per subject.
- Below 65%: detained for the semester ("term not granted"). Student repeats the semester.
- Medical leave: submit certificate within 3 days of returning.
- Participation in college/University events with permission counts as present (OD) with approved list from the faculty coordinator.
- Shortage lists are displayed 10 days before the end of term. Last teaching day for Semester III, V and VII is 21 November 2026.
