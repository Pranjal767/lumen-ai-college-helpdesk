# Examination Pattern (University of Mumbai, B.Tech; illustrative)

## Structure per Theory Course
- Internal Assessment (IA): 20 marks (two internal tests of 20 marks each, average taken)
- End Semester Exam (ESE): 80 marks, 3 hours
- Term Work (TW): 25 marks (lab journals, assignments, mini-project)
- Practical / Oral: 25 marks for labs

## Passing Standard
Pass in each head separately: IA, ESE (min 32/80 i.e. 40%), TW and practical/oral (min 40%). A student must clear all heads.

## Grading (10-point scale)
| Marks | Grade | Grade Point |
|---|---|---|
| 80 and above | O (Outstanding) | 10 |
| 70 to 79 | A | 9 |
| 60 to 69 | B | 8 |
| 55 to 59 | C | 7 |
| 50 to 54 | D | 6 |
| 45 to 49 | E | 5 |
| 40 to 44 | P (Pass) | 4 |
| Below 40 | F (Fail) | 0 |

## SGPI and CGPI
- SGPI = sum of (credits x grade points) / total credits in the semester.
- CGPI = cumulative over all semesters.
- Percentage equivalent: 7.1 x CGPI + 11 (as notified by MU).

## Degree Class
CGPI 7.75 and above: First Class with Distinction; 6.75 to 7.74: First Class; 6.25 to 6.74: Higher Second Class; 5.75 to 6.24: Second Class; 4.0 to 5.74: Pass.
