# Examination FAQ
**When do end semester exams start?** 2 December 2026 and run until 18 December 2026.
**When are the practical exams?** 23 to 30 November 2026.
**When are hall tickets available?** From 25 November 2026.
**Who do I contact if my name is missing from the exam list?** Exam Cell, Room A-120, Prof. Pooja Mehta, exams@aurora-tech.example.
**What is the passing mark?** 40% in each of IA, ESE, TW and practical/oral.
**Can I give an exam without the exam form?** No. The form is mandatory.
**What if I miss an exam due to illness?** Contact the Exam Cell within 24 hours with a medical certificate; absence is treated as ATKT attempt as per MU rules; no special exam is guaranteed.
**When will results be out?** By 22 January 2027 (expected, MU decides).
