# Notice: Exam Forms for End Semester Exams, December 2026

Exam forms for Semesters III, V and VII (regular and ATKT) must be filled on the AIT Exam Portal.

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Form filling starts | 12 October 2026 |
| Last date without fine | 31 October 2026 |
| Last date with fine of Rs. 500 | 7 November 2026 |
| After 7 November | With Principal's approval and fine of Rs. 2,000 (closed now; contact Exam Cell) |

## Exam Fee
- Regular candidates: Rs. 1,500 per semester
- ATKT candidates: Rs. 450 per theory head, Rs. 300 per practical head
- Fee must be paid online along with the form. Eligible only if attendance is 75% or more and fees are cleared.
