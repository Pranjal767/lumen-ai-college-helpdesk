# Marksheets and Degree
- Grade card is available from MU through the portal and AIT; the original gradecard is distributed by AIT within 15 days of result.
- Provisional degree certificate: apply on MU portal after the final semester results; AIT issues bonafide for submission.
- Degree convocation: MU convocation is held once a year (usually in February to April).
- Transcript: Rs. 500, ready in 7 working days. Duplicate marksheet needs FIR/affidavit and MU fee.
- Migration certificate: apply to MU through college (fee Rs. 600).
- Verification (for jobs/higher studies): Exam Cell sends results to the verifying agency, Rs. 1,000 per request.
