# Hall Ticket and Rules
## Hall Ticket
- Download from AIT Student Portal > Examination > Hall Ticket from **25 November 2026**.
- Not issued if attendance is below 75% (without condonation) or fees are pending.
- Hall ticket and college ID card are mandatory every day.

## Exam Hall Rules
- Report 30 minutes before start (10:00 AM). Entry allowed up to 11:00 AM; no exit before 12:00 noon.
- Allowed: pen, pencil, scientific calculator (non-programmable). Not allowed: mobile phones, smartwatches, earphones, bags, notes.
- Write your seat number on every page of the answer book.
- Seating plan is displayed 1 day before at the Examination Cell notice board and portal.

## Unfair Means (UFM)
Cases are reported to the University of Mumbai UFM committee. Penalties range from cancellation of the paper, to cancellation of the entire semester and debarment for up to 2 semesters for serious cases.
