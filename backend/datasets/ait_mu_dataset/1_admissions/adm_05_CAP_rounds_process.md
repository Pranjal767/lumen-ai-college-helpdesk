# CAP Process Step by Step
1. Register on the CET Cell portal and fill the application form.
2. Upload documents and get them verified at a Facilitation Centre (FC). Choose an FC near you.
3. Provisional merit list is published; raise grievances in the given window.
4. Final merit list is published.
5. Fill the Option Form: preferences of colleges and branches (AIT code 3456; branch codes appear on the portal).
6. Round allotment: system allots one seat as per merit and preferences.
7. Candidate response after allotment:
   - **Freeze**: accept and take admission; exit CAP.
   - **Betterment/Float/Slide**: accept the seat but stay for upgradation within the same college (slide) or other colleges (float).
   - **Auto-freeze** applies for the first preference.
8. Report to AIT with originals and fee within the reporting dates.

## If You Miss Reporting
The seat is cancelled and offered in the next round; you may need to re-register and pay a re-registration fee as per CET Cell.
