# Cancellation and Refund Policy (as per AICTE norms)

## Withdrawal Refund Schedule
| When | Refund |
|---|---|
| Up to the AICTE cut-off date (30 August 2027) | Full fee refunded; Rs. 1,000 processing fee deducted |
| After cut-off date, before start of Semester II | No refund of tuition; refundable deposits returned |
| After Semester II begins | No refund; refundable deposits returned |
Hostel fee is refunded pro rata for the unused months if the room is vacated and keys returned.

## Procedure
1. Submit a written application signed by student and parent to the Admission Office.
2. Surrender the original documents receipt, ID card and library card.
3. Get the No Dues form signed by Library, Hostel, Accounts and Department.
4. Refund is processed to the payer's bank account within 15 working days. Original documents are returned within 7 working days of the request.

## Seat Surrender in CAP
Students can surrender seats through the CET Cell portal; AIT forwards the cancellation request. Surrender after the last CAP round may result in the loss of the CAP seat acceptance fee.
