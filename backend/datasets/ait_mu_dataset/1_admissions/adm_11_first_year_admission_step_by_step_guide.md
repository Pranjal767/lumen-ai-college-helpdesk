# First-Year B.Tech Admission Guide (Provisional Demo Data)

This is a detailed guide assembled from the fictional AIT admission brochure, CAP-round schedule, document checklist, fee structure, and FAQ. Dates, seats, eligibility thresholds, and procedures are illustrative and provisional until published by Maharashtra State CET Cell / DTE and the college.

## 1. Check eligibility and entrance route

- First-year B.Tech eligibility in the dataset requires HSC (10+2) with Physics and Mathematics plus Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, or a Technical Vocational subject.
- The listed aggregate threshold is 45% in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, or 40% for reserved categories and PwD.
- The dataset lists MHT-CET PCM for Maharashtra State seats and JEE Main for All India seats.
- A candidate with another board or university background may need a University of Mumbai Eligibility Certificate and later a Migration Certificate.
- Candidates should confirm the exact current-year eligibility and subject rules in the State CET Cell information brochure.

## 2. Register for CAP

1. Register on the Maharashtra State CET Cell portal when the 2027-28 CAP window opens.
2. Enter personal, academic, category, candidature, and entrance-exam details carefully; use the same spelling and date of birth as the certificates.
3. Upload legible scans of the required documents and pay the portal's CAP application fee, if applicable.
4. Select a Facilitation Centre for document verification. The dataset lists AIT as FC code 3456, but candidates should verify the active FC list on the portal.
5. Save the application form, payment acknowledgement, document-verification status, and registration number.

## 3. Check provisional and final merit

- Review the provisional merit list and verify name, category, score, and candidature details.
- Submit a grievance with evidence during the official correction window if an entry is wrong.
- Use the final merit number when preparing college and branch preferences.

## 4. Fill and verify the option form

- Rank preferred colleges and branches in genuine preference order. The dataset lists AIT institute code 3456; branch codes are shown on the official option form.
- Verify category / seat type (HU, OHU, State Level, All India, TFWS) and branch before locking or submitting the form.
- A first-preference allotment may be auto-frozen under the CAP rules described in the dataset. Read the current response options before confirming.
- Save a copy or screenshot of the submitted option form and its confirmation.

## 5. Respond to an allotment

- Follow the portal's current instructions and response deadline for the allotted seat.
- The dataset describes Freeze as accepting the seat and exiting CAP; Float / Betterment as accepting while remaining eligible for an upgrade elsewhere; Slide as seeking an upgrade within the same college.
- Do not assume a seat is secured until the portal shows the required acceptance, fee payment, and reporting steps as complete.
- If betterment is chosen, keep track of later round results and any new reporting deadline.

## 6. Report to the college

1. Download the allotment letter and CAP application / acknowledgement.
2. Bring originals plus three sets of self-attested copies from the admission checklist.
3. Complete original-document verification and submit any required undertaking for an allowed pending document.
4. Pay the amount shown in the current written fee demand and retain the receipt.
5. Collect the admission acknowledgement and confirm that the portal and college records show the admission as completed.

## 7. Institute-level / vacancy rounds

The dataset describes institute-level seats after CAP for registered candidates on the CAP merit list, with no management quota. Interested candidates should register as instructed, monitor the vacancy list and merit notices, and follow published reporting and payment dates. The stated 2027 dates are tentative; a student must not rely on a predicted vacancy or an old schedule.
