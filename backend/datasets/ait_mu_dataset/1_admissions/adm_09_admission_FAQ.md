# Admissions FAQ

**Q: Can I get admission without MHT-CET?** Yes, with a valid JEE Main score under All India seats. Seats after CAP are also filled at Institute Level for registered candidates with a score.

**Q: What is the difference between HU and OHU?** HU (Home University) seats go to students who passed HSC from the MU region. OHU is for students from other universities in Maharashtra.

**Q: I studied 12th from CBSE in Delhi. Am I eligible?** Yes, through All India seats with JEE Main. You also need an MU Eligibility Certificate.

**Q: My caste validity is pending. Can I still join?** Yes. Submit the application receipt and an undertaking, then submit the validity within 12 months.

**Q: Are there spot admissions?** Yes, vacant seats are filled at the Institute Level after the last CAP round, until the AICTE cut-off date.

**Q: Is a gap year allowed?** Yes, with a gap affidavit or certificate.

**Q: Can documents be submitted later?** Only the caste validity (12 months) and eligibility certificate (3 months). All other documents are needed at reporting.

**Q: Can I change my branch after admission?** Branch change is allowed after Semester I for the top 10% by CGPA subject to seat availability and DTE rules.

**Q: Is there a management quota?** There is no management quota; 10% Institute Level seats are filled by merit through CET percentile or JEE Main.
