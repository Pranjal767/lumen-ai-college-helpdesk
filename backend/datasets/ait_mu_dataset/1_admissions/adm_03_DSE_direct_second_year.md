# Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE) 2027-28

## Eligibility
- 3-year Diploma in Engineering/Technology (MSBTE or equivalent) with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved categories), OR
- B.Sc. degree with Mathematics as a subject, with 45% (40% reserved). B.Sc. students must pass bridge courses in Engineering Mathematics.

## Admission Process
Through DSE CAP of the State CET Cell. No separate entrance test; merit is based on diploma marks. Candidates register on the CET Cell portal, verify documents at a Facilitation Centre, fill options and join the allotted college.

## Intake
Up to 10% of the sanctioned intake of each branch (lateral entry into Semester III).
| Branch | DSE seats |
|---|---|
| Computer Engineering | 12 |
| Information Technology | 6 |
| AI&DS | 6 |
| EXTC | 6 |
| Mechanical | 6 |
| Civil | 3 |

## Documents (in addition to the standard list)
- Marksheets of all Diploma semesters (I to VI) and final diploma certificate
- SSC marksheet and School Leaving Certificate / Transfer Certificate
- Domicile / Nationality certificate, caste certificates, NCL, income certificate as applicable
- Gap certificate if applicable
- Aadhaar, 6 photographs, medical fitness certificate, anti-ragging affidavit

## Dates (tentative)
DSE CAP registration: July 2027. Classes for DSE students begin: 2 August 2027.
