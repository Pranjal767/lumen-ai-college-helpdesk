# Admission Document Checklist (Illustrative Demo Data)

Bring originals and three sets of self-attested copies as stated in the fictional AIT checklist. Originals are verified and returned except the School Leaving Certificate, which the dataset says the college retains. Check the latest college checklist before reporting.

## Core checklist for every first-year candidate

- SSC / Class 10 marksheet for date-of-birth verification.
- HSC / Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate.
- Printed MHT-CET or JEE Main scorecard, as applicable.
- CAP allotment letter, CAP application form, and acknowledgement / receipt.
- Original School Leaving Certificate or Transfer Certificate.
- Domicile or Nationality certificate where required for Maharashtra candidature.
- Aadhaar card and copy; six recent colour passport photographs.
- Medical fitness certificate in the college format.
- Student and parent anti-ragging affidavit submitted through the UGC portal.
- Student bank passbook / cancelled cheque with Aadhaar-linked account details for DBT schemes.

## Add documents for a reserved-category / concession claim

- Caste certificate from the competent authority for the claimed category.
- Caste Validity Certificate. If it is still being processed, the dataset allows the acknowledgement receipt and undertaking, with validity submitted within 12 months; confirm this allowance in the current notice.
- Non-Creamy Layer certificate for categories that require it; the dataset's 2027-28 checklist says valid through 31 March 2028 or later.
- EWS certificate valid for the notified financial year, if applicable.
- Tahsildar income certificate for TFWS, Freeship, EBC, or scholarship claims, where required.
- Disability certificate from the authorised medical board for a PwD claim.
- Defence, orphan, or Kashmiri migrant certificate for the applicable claim.

## Add documents for board, domicile, gap, or international cases

- CBSE / ICSE / IB / other-board or out-of-university candidates: University of Mumbai Eligibility Certificate; the dataset says to apply within three months of admission. Submit the Migration Certificate before university enrollment.
- Gap-year candidates: gap certificate or affidavit explaining the gap period.
- NRI / foreign candidates: passport, visa, AIU Equivalence Certificate, and sponsor letter; confirm current additional requirements with Admissions.
- Name mismatch: gazette notification or affidavit matching the education records.

## Direct Second Year diploma / B.Sc. candidates

In addition to applicable core documents, the dataset lists all Diploma semester marksheets (I–VI) and final diploma certificate, or B.Sc. degree and marksheets. B.Sc. candidates should verify the Mathematics / bridge-course requirement. Add domicile and category documents, gap certificate, Aadhaar, photographs, medical fitness, and anti-ragging documents as applicable.

## Postgraduate candidates

The dataset lists degree marksheets for all semesters, degree or provisional certificate, applicable entrance scorecard, gap certificate if required, and optional work-experience letter for MMS applicants, in addition to standard identity and category documents.

## Before leaving for reporting

1. Match the name, date of birth, category, and marks across all documents.
2. Put originals in one folder and three copy sets in separate labelled folders.
3. Keep digital scans and CAP / entrance login details available.
4. Confirm which originals the institution retains and obtain an acknowledgement.
5. Confirm the current admission office hours and whether an appointment is needed.
