# Postgraduate Programs at AIT

| Program | Duration | Intake | Entrance |
|---|---|---|---|
| MMS (MBA equivalent) | 2 years | 60 | MAH-MBA/MMS CET, CAT, or other national tests via CAP |
| MCA | 2 years (post-graduation) | 60 | MAH-MCA CET via CAP |
| M.E. Computer Engineering | 2 years | 18 | GATE / DTE CAP for M.E. |

## Eligibility
- MMS: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with at least 50% (45% reserved).
- MCA: Bachelor's degree (BCA / B.Sc. / B.Com / BA with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation) with at least 50% (45% reserved).
- M.E.: B.E./B.Tech in Computer/IT/relevant branch with at least 50%.

## Documents
All standard FE documents plus: degree marksheets of all semesters, degree certificate or provisional certificate, entrance scorecard, GAP certificate if required, and work experience letter (MMS, optional).

## Dates
MMS and MCA CAP: July to August 2027. M.E. CAP: August 2027. Classes begin: September 2027.
