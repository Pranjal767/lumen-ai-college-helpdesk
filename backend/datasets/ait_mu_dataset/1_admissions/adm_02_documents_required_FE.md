# Documents Required for Admission (FE B.Tech)
Bring originals plus 3 sets of self-attested photocopies. Originals are verified and returned, except the School Leaving Certificate (kept by college).

## A. Mandatory for All Candidates
1. SSC (Class 10) marksheet (proof of date of birth)
2. HSC (Class 12) marksheet and passing certificate
3. MHT-CET scorecard / JEE Main scorecard (printed)
4. CAP allotment letter and CAP registration form (printouts) with acknowledgement receipt
5. School Leaving Certificate (original) or Transfer Certificate from the last college/school
6. Domicile certificate or Nationality certificate (for Maharashtra State candidature)
7. Aadhaar card (original and copy)
8. 6 passport-size photographs (recent, colour)
9. Medical fitness certificate (college format)
10. Anti-Ragging affidavit (online, from UGC antiragging portal) signed by student and parent
11. Bank passbook / cancelled cheque of student's account (Aadhaar linked, for DBT scholarships)

## B. Category-Specific Documents
- Caste certificate (SC / ST / VJ / NT-B / NT-C / NT-D / OBC / SBC / SEBC): from Competent Authority.
- Caste Validity Certificate: original from Scrutiny Committee. If not yet received, submit acknowledgement receipt of the application plus an undertaking to submit the validity within 12 months.
- Non-Creamy Layer Certificate: for OBC / VJ / NT / SBC / SEBC. For 2027-28 admission it must be valid up to 31 March 2028 (or later).
- EWS Certificate: for EWS category, valid for the financial year as notified.
- Income Certificate (Tahsildar): for TFWS, Freeship and scholarships; shows income of the previous financial year.
- Disability certificate (40% or more) from Civil Surgeon / authorised medical board: for PwD.
- Defence quota: certificate from Zilla Sainik Board / Defence authority.
- Orphan certificate, Kashmiri migrant certificate: as applicable.

## C. Special Cases
- Candidates from CBSE/ICSE/IB or other state boards: Eligibility Certificate from University of Mumbai (apply within 3 months of admission; AIT helps via MU Eligibility portal). Migration certificate is submitted before the university enrollment.
- Gap year candidates: gap certificate / affidavit explaining the gap.
- NRI / Foreign nationals: passport, visa, Equivalence Certificate (AIU), sponsor letter.
- Name change / mismatch: gazette notification or affidavit.

## D. Pay at Reporting
Allotment acceptance fee (as per CAP rules) and first installment of fees (see Fee Structure).
