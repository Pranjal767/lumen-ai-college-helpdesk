# First Year B.Tech Admissions 2027-28 (provisional, subject to DTE Maharashtra / State CET Cell notifications)

## Admission Route
Admissions are through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) run by the State CET Cell, Maharashtra.
- Qualifying entrance: MHT-CET (PCM group) score for Maharashtra State seats; JEE Main score for All India seats.
- Seats: Home University (HU) seats are 70% of State Level seats (candidates who passed HSC from the MU region: Mumbai, Mumbai Suburban, Thane, Palghar, Raigad, Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg). Other Than Home University (OHU) seats are 30%.
- All India Quota: 15% of seats filled by JEE Main merit.
- TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme): 5% of seats.
- Institute Level seats: filled by AIT after CAP rounds from the CAP merit list and registered candidates.

## Eligibility
- Passed HSC (10+2) with Physics and Mathematics, plus Chemistry / Biotechnology / Biology / Technical Vocational subject.
- Minimum 45% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (40% for reserved categories and PwD).
- Valid MHT-CET 2027 (PCM) or JEE Main 2027 score.
