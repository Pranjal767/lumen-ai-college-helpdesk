# CAP Choices and Allotment Responses (Illustrative Demo Data)

The terms below summarize the fictional dataset's description of Maharashtra engineering CAP. The current State CET Cell brochure and portal instructions override this explanation.

## Option form

The option form is the ranked list of college / branch choices used by the allotment system. Put choices in the order you would actually accept them; the first choice should be the most preferred. Confirm the institute code, branch code, seat type, and category for each choice before final submission, and save the confirmation.

## Responses described in the dataset

| Response | Meaning in the dataset | Practical implication |
|---|---|---|
| Freeze | Accept the allotted seat and exit CAP | You stop competing for an upgrade in later rounds. |
| Float / Betterment | Accept the seat but remain eligible for an upgrade at another college | Track subsequent allotments and reporting rules carefully. |
| Slide | Accept the seat and seek an upgrade to another branch in the same college | A branch change depends on merit, available seats, and the current CAP process. |
| Auto-freeze | The dataset says this may apply to a first-preference allotment | Read the current portal notice; do not assume the same setting every year. |

## After an allotment

1. Check the allotment status, reporting deadline, and required acceptance payment on the CAP portal.
2. Choose the response available for that round and confirm it before the deadline.
3. Download the allotment letter and fee / acceptance acknowledgement.
4. Complete the college reporting and original-document verification steps.
5. If eligible for an upgrade, check later results and new deadlines; an earlier allotment does not remove the need to follow portal instructions.

Missing a reporting deadline can result in seat cancellation or loss of a later-round opportunity. The dataset says the seat may be offered in the next round and re-registration may be required; confirm the current rule on the portal.

## Institute-level / vacancy rounds

The dataset describes these rounds as filling vacant seats after CAP from registered candidates on the CAP merit list. It states there is no management quota. Candidates should follow the college's published vacancy list, merit order, registration instructions, fees, and cut-off date. Never treat a phone call, unofficial message, or previous-year vacancy as a confirmed seat.
