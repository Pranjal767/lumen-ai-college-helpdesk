Monday to Friday: regular timetable. Saturday: 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM for mentoring, co-curricular activities and remedial classes; Sundays closed.

## Divisions and Batches
Each year has 2 to 3 divisions (A, B, C) of about 60 students. For practicals each division is split into three batches (A1, A2, A3) of 20 students.

## Where to find the timetable
Student Portal > Academics > Timetable, and department notice boards. Room allocation changes are announced on the portal and the department WhatsApp group.
