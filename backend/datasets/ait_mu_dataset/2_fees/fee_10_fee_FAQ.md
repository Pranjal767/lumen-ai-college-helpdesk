# Fee FAQ
**How do I pay fees?** Via Student Portal > Accounts > Pay Fees, or at the counter.
**Is there a late fee for scholarship students?** No fine if the scholarship application is pending and an undertaking has been submitted.
**Can I pay the fee in installments other than two?** Not normally. Hardship cases can apply to the Principal for 3 installments, with Rs. 1,000 processing fee.
**Which receipt do I use for the education loan?** Provisional fee structure and receipts from the portal. Loan disbursement from the bank is paid to AIT's account: State Bank of India, Vashi branch.
**Is GST charged?** No, educational services are exempt.
**Can another person pay?** Yes, but the receipt will be in the student's name.
