# No Dues
A student needs a No Dues certificate for: exam form submission, hall ticket, final year degree, internship NOC, refund of deposits.

## Departments that sign
Library, Hostel (if applicable), Accounts, Laboratory (dept), Training and Placement, Student Section.

## Process
Apply on Student Portal > Certificates > No Dues. Each department approves online in 2 working days. Final certificate is issued by Accounts.

## Fee Clearance Rules
- Pending fees block the exam form and hall ticket.
- Scholarship-pending students may submit an undertaking (Student Welfare Office) to take hall tickets.
