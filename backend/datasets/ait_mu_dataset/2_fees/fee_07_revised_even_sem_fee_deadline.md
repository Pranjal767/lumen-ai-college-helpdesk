# Notice: Revised Even Semester Fee Deadline
Supersedes the notice dated 5 October 2026 (which stated 31 December 2026).

The last date to pay the second installment (50% of annual fee) for the even semester (Jan to May 2027) is now **15 January 2027** without late fine. The date has been extended because the odd semester exams end on 18 December and the winter break runs until 3 January 2027.

Late fine will apply from 16 January 2027 as per the Fee Payment Rules.
Students waiting for scholarship reimbursement can submit an undertaking at the Student Welfare Office.
