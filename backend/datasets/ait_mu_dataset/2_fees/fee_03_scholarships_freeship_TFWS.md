# Scholarships and Fee Concessions (indicative, verify on mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in)

## TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme)
- 5% of seats; family annual income up to Rs. 8,00,000.
- Documents: income certificate, domicile, CAP allotment under TFWS.
- Tuition fee waived; other fees payable. Continues if student does not have an ATKT beyond rules.

## Freeship (SC / ST / VJNT / OBC / SBC / SEBC)
- Government reimbursement of tuition and exam fee based on category and income limits.
- Apply on MahaDBT portal every year (fresh and renewal).
- SC/ST students: AIT does not collect tuition fee, but admits against income/caste documents; non-reimbursable fees (hostel, bus) are payable.

## EBC (Economically Backward Class)
- 50% concession on tuition for Open category students with family income up to Rs. 8 lakh, as per Government rules.

## Hostel Maintenance Allowance
Eligible students of reserved categories can claim hostel maintenance allowance through the Government scheme (apply on MahaDBT).

## AIT Scholarships
1. **AIT Merit Award:** Top 10 students by CGPA in each year get Rs. 10,000.
2. **Aurora Girls in Tech Grant:** Rs. 15,000 per year for 10 female students with CGPA 8.0 or above and income below Rs. 6 lakh.
3. **Sports Excellence Award:** Rs. 10,000 for University/State level medalists.

## How to Apply on MahaDBT
1. Register with Aadhaar and mobile number.
2. Fill the scholarship form and upload documents: caste certificate, validity, income certificate, previous marksheet, fee receipt, bank passbook.
3. Take a printout and submit it to the Student Welfare Office for verification (Room A-110).
4. The college verifies and forwards. Money is credited to the student's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Last date for fresh applications is usually 31 October to 30 November; see the latest notice.
