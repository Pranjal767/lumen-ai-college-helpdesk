# Notice: MahaDBT Scholarship Applications 2026-27
All SC/ST/OBC/VJNT/SBC/SEBC and EBC students must complete MahaDBT applications and submit the printout with documents to the Student Welfare Office (Room A-110).

- Last date for submission of printout at college: **30 November 2026**
- Renewal students (SE, TE, BE) must attach the previous year's marksheet and fee receipt.
- Verification timings: 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Students whose applications are not verified by the deadline will not get scholarships for this year.
