# How Fee Concessions Apply (Illustrative Demo Data)

All amounts and eligibility examples below come from the fictional AIT demo fee records. Government scheme rules and current MahaDBT / CET Cell notices control actual eligibility and reimbursement.

## Start with the fee components

The annual institutional total has three components: tuition, development fee, and other institutional fee. A concession described as tuition-only does not automatically remove development, institutional, university, examination, hostel, mess, or transport charges.

## TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme)

- The dataset lists 5% of seats under TFWS and a family annual income limit of up to Rs. 8,00,000.
- It describes 100% tuition waiver, not a waiver of every fee.
- Development and other institutional fees remain payable in the listed structure, as do applicable university, examination, deposit, hostel, mess, or transport charges.
- The listed supporting documents are income certificate, domicile, and CAP allotment under TFWS.
- Seat allotment under TFWS and continuing eligibility are subject to the current CET Cell / government rules.

## Freeship and post-matric scholarship

- The dataset describes tuition and examination-fee reimbursement for eligible SC / ST and other listed reserved categories, with the scheme and income conditions varying by category.
- It says SC/ST students should provide income and caste documents; non-reimbursable charges such as hostel and bus remain payable.
- Applications and renewals are made through MahaDBT each year. The listed supporting records include caste certificate and validity, income certificate, previous marksheet, fee receipt, and bank passbook.
- The Student Welfare Office verifies submitted copies before forwarding the application. Reimbursement timing and amount are governed by the scheme, not guaranteed by this illustrative dataset.

## Documents and practical steps

1. Check the specific scheme and current income/category conditions on the current government portal.
2. Keep the admission allotment, domicile, income certificate, caste certificate and validity (where applicable), prior-year marksheet, fee receipt, Aadhaar-linked bank details, and any scheme-specific forms ready.
3. Submit the online application and a copy to the Student Welfare Office for college verification.
4. Keep the application number and proof of submission. Ask Accounts how any pending reimbursement affects the amount payable by the institute deadline; do not assume a waiver until approved.
5. Keep receipts for every payment and retain the approval / disbursement status for reconciliation.

## NRI and foreign-student rates

The fee record lists USD 6,500 per year for NRI and USD 8,500 per year for foreign students. It does not define all included components or exchange-rate handling, so confirm the written quotation with Admissions / Accounts before paying.
