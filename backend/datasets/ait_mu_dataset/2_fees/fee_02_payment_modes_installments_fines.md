# Fee Payment Rules

## Modes
- Online on AIT Student Portal: UPI, net banking, debit/credit card
- Demand Draft or Cheque in favor of "Aurora Institute of Technology" payable at Navi Mumbai
- Cash at Accounts counter up to Rs. 20,000 (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday)

## Installments (annual fee)
- 1st installment (50%): at admission, no later than 31 August
- 2nd installment (50%): before start of even semester (see latest notice for date)
Hostel and bus fees are paid in full at the start of the academic year.

## Late Fine
| Delay | Fine |
|---|---|
| 1 to 7 days | Rs. 100 per day |
| 8 to 15 days | Rs. 200 per day |
| More than 15 days | Rs. 5,000, plus Principal's approval to continue |
Students with unpaid fees cannot fill exam forms, download hall tickets or collect marksheets.

## Receipts
Download from Student Portal > Accounts > Receipts. Duplicate hard copy: Rs. 100. Fee receipts give 80C tax benefit; tax certificate is available from the Accounts Office in April.
