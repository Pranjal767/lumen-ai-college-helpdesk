# Fee Payment and Clearance Workflow (Illustrative Demo Data)

Use the current Student Portal notice and Accounts Office demand as the final authority. This document combines the fictional fee dataset's payment, installment, late-charge, receipt, and No Dues information.

## Before paying

1. Confirm the academic year, program, branch, student category, and whether the demand is for tuition, development / institutional charges, a university fee, examination, or an optional service.
2. Review the current written demand. Compare concessions only after checking the approved scheme and submitted documents.
3. Check the due date and installment amount in the current portal notice; older notices may be superseded.

## Listed payment methods

- Student Portal > Accounts > Pay Fees: UPI, net banking, debit card, or credit card.
- Demand Draft or cheque in favour of “Aurora Institute of Technology”, payable at Navi Mumbai.
- Cash at the Accounts counter is listed up to Rs. 20,000, Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
- A parent or another person may pay; the receipt remains in the student's name.

## Installments and late charges

The dataset describes two installments of 50% of annual fees: the first at admission and the second before the even semester, with the exact second deadline set by the latest notice. It also contains a FAQ saying a hardship request for three installments may be submitted to the Principal with a Rs. 1,000 processing fee; this is an exception request, not an entitlement, and must be confirmed in writing.

The listed late charges are Rs. 100 per day for days 1–7, Rs. 200 per day for days 8–15, and Rs. 5,000 after 15 days plus Principal approval to continue. The records also state that unpaid dues can block exam-form submission, hall-ticket download, or marksheet collection. Confirm the current rule and any approved scholarship-pending undertaking before relying on an exception.

## Receipts and education loans

- Download a receipt from Student Portal > Accounts > Receipts after payment.
- The dataset lists Rs. 100 for a duplicate hard copy.
- Keep the portal receipt, transaction reference, and bank confirmation. If a payment is not reflected, contact Accounts with the transaction ID before paying again.
- For an education loan, the dataset lists the provisional fee structure and portal receipt as supporting documents. The exact bank disbursement process should be confirmed with the bank and Accounts.
- The FAQ states that education services are GST-exempt and that another person may pay while the receipt remains in the student's name; verify the applicable current receipt / tax treatment with Accounts.

## No Dues and clearance

The dataset lists the No Dues requirement for exam forms, hall tickets, degree completion, internship NOC, and deposit refunds. The listed departments are Library, Hostel (if applicable), Accounts, department laboratory, Training and Placement, and Student Section.

1. Apply on Student Portal > Certificates > No Dues.
2. Resolve each department's pending item and track online approvals.
3. Accounts issues the final certificate after approvals.
4. Keep a copy of the clearance and deposit-refund request.

The dataset says departments target approval within two working days; processing time may vary and is not guaranteed.
