# Refund Policy
Refund is as per AICTE norms (see Admission Cancellation notice).
- Withdrawal up to the AICTE cut-off date: full refund less Rs. 1,000.
- After cut-off: tuition not refunded; deposits refunded.
- Caution deposit and library deposit are refunded within 30 days after graduation or withdrawal on submission of the No Dues certificate.
- Excess payments or payments made twice are refunded in 15 working days on application to the Accounts Office with the bank transaction ID.
- Scholarship reimbursement received by the college is adjusted or refunded to students as per the scheme.
