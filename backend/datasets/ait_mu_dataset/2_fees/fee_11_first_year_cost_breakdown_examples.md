# First-Year Engineering Fee Breakdown (Illustrative Demo Data)

This guide combines the 2026-27 branch fee table with the listed one-time charges. It is a calculation aid for the fictional AIT demo dataset, not a fee demand or official quotation. The current Fee Regulating Authority order, CET Cell rules, and the college's written fee notice take precedence.

## Annual institutional fee by branch

The annual institutional total is tuition + development fee + other institutional fee. The listed other institutional fee covers library, gymkhana, ID card, laboratory, student insurance, and internet.

| Branch group | Tuition | Development | Other institutional | Annual institutional total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Computer Engineering, AI&DS, Information Technology | Rs. 1,45,000 | Rs. 12,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,65,000 |
| EXTC | Rs. 1,30,000 | Rs. 12,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,50,000 |
| Mechanical Engineering | Rs. 1,15,000 | Rs. 12,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,35,000 |
| Civil Engineering | Rs. 1,05,000 | Rs. 12,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,25,000 |

## Listed first-year one-time charges

- Caution deposit: Rs. 5,000, refundable under the stated refund conditions.
- Library security deposit: Rs. 2,000, refundable under the stated refund conditions.
- University enrollment fee: Rs. 1,000, one time.
- University eligibility fee: Rs. 600 one time for students from outside the University of Mumbai / Maharashtra Board, where applicable.

Adding the two refundable deposits and the enrollment fee to the annual institutional fee gives these illustrative first-year totals before optional services, examination charges, and any applicable eligibility fee:

| Branch group | Annual institutional fee | Listed deposits + enrollment | First-year subtotal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Computer Engineering, AI&DS, Information Technology | Rs. 1,65,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,73,000 |
| EXTC | Rs. 1,50,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,58,000 |
| Mechanical Engineering | Rs. 1,35,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,43,000 |
| Civil Engineering | Rs. 1,25,000 | Rs. 8,000 | Rs. 1,33,000 |

Add Rs. 600 only if the University eligibility fee applies. Do not treat this subtotal as including exam fees, hostel, mess, bus, or scholarship adjustments; their amounts or applicability are separate.

## Installment examples

The payment note describes two installments of 50% each on the annual fee. On the listed annual institutional fee alone, that is Rs. 82,500 per installment for Computer / AI&DS / IT, Rs. 75,000 for EXTC, Rs. 67,500 for Mechanical, and Rs. 62,500 for Civil. Deposits and one-time university charges are additional line items; the exact payment demand and due date must be confirmed with Accounts.

## Optional services listed separately

- Hostel, double sharing without mess: Rs. 48,000 per year.
- Hostel, triple sharing without mess: Rs. 40,000 per year.
- Hostel mess: Rs. 42,000 per year.
- College bus pass: Rs. 20,000 per year.
- Hostel security deposit: Rs. 5,000 refundable.

These options should not be added automatically to a student's college-fee estimate. Ask which service is selected and check the current written charge notice.
