## University of Mumbai Fees (paid via college)
- Enrollment fee: Rs. 1,000 (one time, first year)
- Eligibility fee (students from outside MU/Maharashtra Board): Rs. 600 (one time)
- Exam fee: see Examination Form notice

## By Category (Tuition)
| Category | Fee Treatment |
|---|---|
| Open | Full fee |
| EBC (Open, family income up to Rs. 8 lakh) | 50% of tuition fee concession through Government scheme (via MahaDBT) |
| TFWS | 100% tuition fee waived; other fees payable |
| SC / ST | Tuition and exam fee reimbursed through Freeship / Post Matric scholarship |
| OBC / SEBC / VJ / NT | Fee concession through Government scholarship subject to income limit (see scholarships document) |
| NRI / Foreign | USD 6,500 per year (NRI), USD 8,500 (Foreign) |

## One-Time Refundable Deposits
- Caution deposit: Rs. 5,000
- Library security deposit: Rs. 2,000
