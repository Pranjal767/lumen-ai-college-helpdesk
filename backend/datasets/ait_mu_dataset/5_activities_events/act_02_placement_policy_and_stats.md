# Placement Policy
- Eligibility: Final-year students with CGPI 6.0 or above and no live backlog.
- One-offer policy: a student with an offer below 6 LPA can sit for one more company with a higher package ("dream offer").
- Registration is mandatory on the T&P portal; no-show without notice may lead to a 1-drive block.

## Statistics 2025-26
- Eligible students: 320; placed: 280 (87.5%)
- Highest package: Rs. 28 LPA; Average: Rs. 6.2 LPA; Median: Rs. 5.4 LPA
- Top recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Capgemini, Accenture, Wipro, L&T, Cognizant, Deloitte, Persistent
- Internship conversion: 38 students with Pre-Placement Offers

## Internship
Mandatory 4-week internship after Semester VI (May to June); submit report and certificate by 15 July. NOC for internship available from the T&P Cell.

## Training
Aptitude and coding training for SE, TE and BE: Monday to Thursday 4:00 to 5:00 PM. Mock interviews in Semester VII.
