# Aurora Sports Week (11 to 16 January 2027)
Events: Cricket, Football, Kabaddi, Volleyball, Basketball, Badminton, Table Tennis, Chess, Athletics (100 m, 400 m, relay, long jump, shot put). Separate categories for boys and girls.

- Registration: with Physical Director, Mr. Ganesh Sawant (Ground Floor, Sports Room), by 5 January 2027.
- Matches: 7:00 to 9:00 AM and 4:30 to 6:30 PM; classes run normally from 9:00 AM.
- The college teams for MU inter-collegiate tournaments are selected from the performance in the Sports Week.
- MU Zonal tournament dates are announced by the University's Department of Sports, around February to March 2027.
- Facilities: ground, indoor games room, gymnasium (6:30 AM to 8:00 PM).
