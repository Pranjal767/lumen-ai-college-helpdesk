# University of Mumbai Events at AIT

## Indradhanushya (MU Youth Festival)
- Competitions: music, dance, theatre, literary events, fine arts, folk art.
- College round auditions: 12 and 13 January 2027 at AIT (Seminar Hall). Selected students represent AIT in the MU zonal round; dates by MU.
- Register at the Cultural Committee by 5 January 2027. Participants can claim attendance leave (OD).

## Avishkar (Research Convention)
- Categories: Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate, Faculty. Streams include Engineering and Technology.
- College round: 22 January 2027, Seminar Hall.
- Submit a 1-page abstract by 10 January 2027 to the Research Cell (Prof. Anil Bhagat).
- Winners go to the University level and State level Avishkar.
