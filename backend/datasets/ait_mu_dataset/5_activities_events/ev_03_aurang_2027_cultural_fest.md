# Aurang 2027 (18, 19, 20 February 2027)

## Schedule
- Day 1: Inauguration 10 AM, Fashion show, Dance (solo and group)
- Day 2: Singing, Band battle, Mono-act, Photography exhibition
- Day 3: Dramatics (skits), Rangoli, Finale and Celebrity Night (7 PM)

## Auditions
Auditions for stage events: 11 to 15 January 2027 in the Seminar Hall.

## Passes
- AIT students: free entry with ID card
- Outside participants: Rs. 150 for 3 days; Celebrity Night Rs. 300
- Volunteers for organizing teams: apply to Cultural Committee by 31 December 2026.

Contact: Cultural Committee, Prof. Anjali Rane, culturals@aurora-tech.example
