# Transport
- Nearest station: Vashi (Harbour Line), 2.5 km. AIT shuttle bus runs every 20 minutes from Vashi station between 7:30 AM and 6:00 PM.
- College bus: 10 routes across Navi Mumbai, Thane, Panvel and Kharghar. Bus pass Rs. 20,000 per year. First morning bus reaches campus at 8:30 AM; return buses depart at 4:30 PM and 6:00 PM.

# Railway Concession (Mumbai local trains)
- Students can get concession season tickets for Western, Central and Harbour lines.
- Collect the Railway Concession form (signed and stamped) from the Student Section, Room A-103. Processing: same day if submitted before 12:00 noon.
- Required: ID card, fee receipt, passport photo, previous pass (if renewing).
- Valid for the academic year; the form is free.
