# Clubs and Committees

- **IEEE Student Branch** (workshops, talks, Technovate)

- **AI Collective** (ML projects): Thursdays 5 PM, C-301
- **RoboForge** (robotics): Saturdays 10 AM, Innovation Lab

- **ACES Civil Club, ISTE Chapter**

## Cultural and Social
Dramatics, Rhythm (music), Nritya (dance), Lens (photography), Eco Club, E-Cell, Women Development Cell, Marathi Vangmay Mandal.

## Statutory Committees
Anti-Ragging Committee, Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), Grievance Redressal Cell, Student Council.

## How to Join
Register on Student Portal > Student Life > Clubs in the first two weeks of each semester. Recruitment drives are in August and January. No membership fee for most clubs. Activity points: up to 2 per semester.
