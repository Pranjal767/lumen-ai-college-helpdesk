# Hostel
- Boys' Hostel (120 beds) and Girls' Hostel (80 beds) inside the campus, with 24x7 security and CCTV.
- Rooms: double or triple sharing. Fee: see Other Charges (Rs. 48,000 / 40,000 per year without mess).
- In-time: 9:00 PM for first year, 10:00 PM for others. Late permission needs warden approval and parent intimation.
- Visitors: Sunday 10 AM to 5 PM in the visitor lounge.
- Mess: Breakfast 7:30 to 9:00 AM; Lunch 12:30 to 2:00 PM; Snacks 5:00 PM; Dinner 8:00 to 9:30 PM.
- Application: first-year hostel applications close on 15 August; seats by merit and distance from home.
- Facilities: Wi-Fi, reading room, laundry, common room, gym.
- Rector (boys): Prof. Ganesh Patil; Warden (girls): Mrs. Seema Deshmukh.
