# Library
- Timings: Monday to Saturday 8:30 AM to 7:00 PM; Sunday closed. Exam days: 8:30 AM to 11:00 PM, including Sundays.
- Membership: free with ID card; each student can borrow 4 books for 14 days; renewal once.
- Fine: Rs. 2 per day per book.
- Digital library: NPTEL, IEEE, Springer and DELNET through the Student Portal.
- Book bank: SC/ST/Freeship students can borrow a full semester set of books.
- Reading room seats: 150; silence and no mobile rules.
- Librarian: Mrs. Sulabha Kamat.
