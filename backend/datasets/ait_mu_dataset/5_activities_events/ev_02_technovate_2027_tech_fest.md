# Technovate 2027 (5 and 6 February 2027)

## Events
- **CodeStorm:** competitive coding, individual, Day 1, 10 AM to 1 PM
- **Hack-a-thon 24:** 24-hour hackathon, teams of 2 to 4, starts 5 Feb 10:00 AM, ends 6 Feb 10:00 AM. Themes: AI for Education, Smart City, Sustainability.
- **RoboRace:** robotics, teams of up to 4
- **Paper Presentation:** IEEE format, teams of 2
- **Tech Quiz** and **Gaming (Valorant / BGMI)** events
- **Project Expo:** final-year project exhibition, 6 February

## Registration
- Registration fee: Rs. 200 per event for AIT students; Rs. 300 for other colleges; Hackathon Rs. 500 per team.
- Last date to register: 25 January 2027 on the AIT website (technovate.aurora-tech.example).
- Prize pool: Rs. 1,50,000. Hackathon winners: Rs. 50,000, runner-up Rs. 25,000.
- Eligibility: open to all engineering students of colleges in Maharashtra. Certificates for all participants.
- Contact: Prof. Divya Menon (faculty coordinator), student head: Aarav Shah.
