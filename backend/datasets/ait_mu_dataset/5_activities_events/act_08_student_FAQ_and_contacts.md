# FAQ
**How do I get my ID card?** From the Student Section (A-103) within 2 weeks of admission.
**How many activity points do I need?** 100 over 4 years (AICTE activity points), earned through clubs, NSS, workshops and sports.
**How do I get railway concession?** See Transport document; free form from A-103.
**Can I use a bike?** Yes, with a college parking sticker (Rs. 300 per year).
**How do I join Technovate as an organiser?** Apply to the Technical Committee by 30 November.
